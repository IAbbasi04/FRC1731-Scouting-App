# Team 1731 visual theme

The scouting app uses a Team 1731-inspired blue and yellow visual system:

- Primary blue: `#0B5FFF`
- Deep blue: `#063E9E`
- Accent yellow: `#FFD84D`
- Dark navy background: `#07111F`
- Elevated navy surface: `#0D1B2E`
- Light text: `#F7FAFC`

These are app theme values chosen to reflect Team 1731's recognizable blue/yellow identity while preserving readable contrast. They are not presented as an official published brand standard.
